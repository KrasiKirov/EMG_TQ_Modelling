Definitions

Answer key: measured torque − passive torque from the passive trials of the tested session (IES has none: nothing subtracted).
R² per position: 1 = perfect, 0 = as good as predicting the trial's average. MAE: mean absolute error (Nm). Demeaned: each trial's mean removed from both curves (shape only). Bias: mean of prediction − measured.
Spread: over training runs (random seeds); the test trials are always the same. Box = 25–75 %, whiskers = 5–95 %, line = median.
Toes-down: the 2 most negative (plantarflexed) positions of each person.
