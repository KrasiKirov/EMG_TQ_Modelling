1. Balanced positions

What we tried

Make every ankle angle count the same in training. Before, the toes-down angles were only about 1% of the loss, so the model mostly ignored them.

How

Divide each angle’s torque by its swing in session 1, train, then multiply the prediction back.

Result

Toes-down error about 30% lower than last semester (0.85 → 0.61 Nm).

6 people · trained on session 1, tested on session 2

At the toes-down angles the torque barely moves, so with a normal loss those angles were about 1% of training. We divide each angle’s torque by how much it swings in session 1, so every angle counts the same, 12.5% each. Result: toes-down error goes from 0.85 to 0.61 Nm, about 30% lower than last semester. Other angles are about 20% lower. Five of six people improve; JM stays about the same.
