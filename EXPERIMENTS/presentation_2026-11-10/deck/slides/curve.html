The calibration line

Fit torque = a × prediction + b on the first 10 s, then use the same a and b for the rest of the trial. Example: YES, −0.381 rad.

One real trial. On the left, each dot is a moment in the first 10 seconds: the model’s prediction against the measured torque. A perfect model would sit on the dashed diagonal. We fit a straight line through the dots: a stretches or shrinks the prediction, b shifts it. Here a is 0.87 and b is minus 0.24 Nm. On the right, the same two numbers are applied to the rest of the trial; orange is before, teal after. We only score from 30 seconds on, so the calibration never grades itself. In this trial the error goes from 0.66 to 0.45 Nm.
