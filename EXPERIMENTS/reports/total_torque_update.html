What we're testing

Our models currently predict active torque (measured minus a passive-table estimate). Errors in the passive table and passive levels that shift between sessions may be hurting results. This study instead trains on measured total torque, with no passive subtraction. Everything else follows D01: four EMG envelopes plus ankle angle, 200 ms windows, balanced positions, the same LSTM and training rules, matched seeds, and no auto-gain. Early stopping uses source-session validation only. We are not assuming this will help.

  • Pre-run checks pass on YES, JM and EG (both directions, all four methods). They confirm that:
    • the target equals processed measured torque;
    • inputs line up with the target in time;
    • scales and balancing factors use training data only;
    • auto-gain is off;
    • predictions are converted back to Nm.
