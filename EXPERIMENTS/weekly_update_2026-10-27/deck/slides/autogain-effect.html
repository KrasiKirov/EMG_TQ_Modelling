Here is the same idea across everyone. Each dot is one ankle angle: how big the calf EMG is in session 2 for the same push, relative to session 1, so 1× means the same size. Blue is before auto-gain, with the EMG divided by the session-1 maximum; orange is after. Before, sessions differ a lot, from about 0.4× for JM to over 2× for EG at one angle. After auto-gain the dots sit much closer to 1× for every person. It is not perfect: a few angles still differ, and averaging the three calf muscles can hide muscles moving in opposite directions, like EG at −0.74 rad. CH is shown faded because auto-gain is switched off for CH.
