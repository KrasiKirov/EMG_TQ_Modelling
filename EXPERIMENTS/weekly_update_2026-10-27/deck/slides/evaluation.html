Each person’s model learns only from session 1: the first 80% of every trial for training, the last 20% (after a one-second gap) to decide when to stop. It is then scored on session 2, all 8 angles, which it has never seen. Training uses every 5th window, batch 64, up to 60 epochs, stopping after 12 epochs without improvement. Scores are per angle, R² and MAE in newton-metres, against the active torque. We trained 10 seeds per person, 60 models in all. Three data fixes made all six people usable: four YES retest pushes were labelled “passive” in the file and are restored; IES has no passive trials, so we use the passive table from the lab’s metadata document for both sessions; and CH was added to the subject list. Results come later, in the dashboard.
