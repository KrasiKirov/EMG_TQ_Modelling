This is the same picture as slide 3, same person (EG), same angle (−0.04 rad), so you can compare box by box. Everything not highlighted is unchanged: the raw recording, the filters, the passive trials and passive maps (session 1’s map builds the training target, session 2’s own map builds the scoring target, exactly as last week), and the LSTM itself, two layers of 8 units. What changes is in teal. First, auto-gain: instead of dividing each muscle by one fixed session-1 maximum, each muscle is rescaled from its own recent past, so in session 2 it adapts to that session’s signal size. The next slide zooms into exactly this box. Second, the windows are 0.2 s, 20 steps, instead of 0.5 s. Third, the loss is balanced: each angle’s target is divided by how much the torque swings at that angle in session 1 (10.9 Nm here, about 2 Nm at the most toes-down angle), so every angle counts the same in training; predictions are multiplied back afterwards. Fourth, a bigger training budget: every window instead of every 5th, batch 8, up to 200 epochs, stopping after 25 epochs without improvement. For this one angle and one seed, the score goes from R² 0.98 and MAE 1.1 Nm last week to R² 0.99 and MAE 0.8 Nm. That is one example only; the full comparison over all people, angles and seeds is in the dashboard.
