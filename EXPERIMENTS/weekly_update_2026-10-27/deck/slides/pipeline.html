4

Step by step. The raw EMG is noisy and fast, so we turn it into an envelope: band-pass 30 to 300 Hz where the muscle signal lives, rectify, and smooth below 2 Hz. We keep every 10th sample (100 Hz) and divide each muscle by its maximum in session 1. The model input is the last half second: 50 samples of the four muscles plus the ankle angle. A small LSTM, two layers of 8 units with 30% dropout, outputs the torque at the end of that window. It slides along every 10 ms, and each person has their own model.
