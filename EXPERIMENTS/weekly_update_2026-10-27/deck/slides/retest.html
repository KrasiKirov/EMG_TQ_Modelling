This slide answers one question: what do we compare the session-2 predictions against? The model predicts active torque, measured torque minus the resting (passive) torque at that angle. The resting torque is not identical between sessions, so last week’s method re-measures it in session 2 and uses that for the session-2 answer key. Panel 1 is a real session-2 trial from EG. Panel 2 zooms on a moment where the muscles are relaxed: the measured torque sits at −2.1 Nm, session 2’s passive map gives −2.6 Nm and session 1’s gives −3.4 Nm, so the session-2 map is the closer reference here; the two maps differ by 0.8 Nm. Panel 3 is the target after subtracting the session-2 value. To be fair, the session-2 map is not closer at every angle: during push trials the relaxed level can also sit below both maps, for example at YES’s most toes-down angle. That is part of what the results will show.
