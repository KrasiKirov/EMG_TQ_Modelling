The new method keeps the same idea, one small recurrent model per person that learns from session 1 and is scored on session 2, but changes two things on purpose: how the muscle signals are scaled (auto-gain instead of a fixed session-1 maximum) and how the training loss treats the angles (every angle counts the same). To be transparent, three other settings also differ because they come with the new pipeline: it uses a 0.2 s input history instead of 0.5 s, a much larger training budget, and its passive maps are built from the still holds in the same spirit. When we compare results, keep in mind that these come along.
