The data: six people, each recorded in two sessions, test then retest. In each session the device holds the ankle at 8 angles, and at each angle the person pushes up and down for 90 seconds following a visual cue. We record four muscles (three calf muscles that push the toes down, and the tibialis anterior on the shin that pulls them up), the ankle angle and the torque, at 1 kHz. The figure is a real 24-second stretch from HM in session 1. The model looks at the shaded half-second of muscle activity and predicts the torque at its end, the dot.
